Donation receipt vs Form 10BE

Donation receipt: issued automatically after your payment is verified. It acknowledges your donation and is not an 80G tax certificate.

Form 10BE: the statutory 80G certificate. The Trust files Form 10BD on the Income Tax portal and can issue Form 10BE only after the portal processes it. It is never generated by this website.

Being eligible for 80G reporting does not guarantee a deduction. Deductions under section 80G depend on the Income-tax Act, the donor's tax regime (not available under the new regime) and the donor's own circumstances. Please consult your tax advisor.
